<h2><span class="en">About</span>About the Lab</h2>

The Goshima Lab at Yokohama National University (YNU) conducts research on **Finance × Data Science**. In addition to methods based on econometrics and statistics, we combine methods from computer science such as natural language processing, machine learning, and deep learning to conduct empirical research on financial data.

The lab supervises undergraduate students in the College of Business Administration and graduate students in the Graduate School of International Social Sciences and the Interfaculty Graduate School of Innovative and Practical Studies.
